1. Image and landmarks

Start by uploading a top-down image

First tap the middle of the forehead, then the middle of the occiput. Next tap around the outer cranial contour, in order, with at least 12 points (ideally 20–35). Drag any existing point to refine it. The software calculates intersections with the contour. Keep hair and ears outside the cranial contour.

Capture guidance

  1. Use a directly overhead photo, with the camera perpendicular to the head plane. Avoid perspective and lens distortion, and keep the full head visible.
  2. Orient the forehead toward the top of the screen where practical. Mark the midline from forehead to occiput.
  3. Trace the cranial vault contour, not the ear or hair outline. The outer bony contour may be difficult to distinguish in photos.
  4. For follow-up, repeat camera angle, distance, head orientation, landmark rules and examiner technique.
A photograph is not a calibrated 3D cranial scan or caliper measurement. Indices here are exploratory 2D estimates and have not been clinically validated. Not for diagnostic decisions, helmet eligibility or ruling out craniosynostosis.

2. Measurement details

CVAI = |diagonal A − diagonal B| ÷ shorter diagonal × 100. CI = transverse width ÷ AP length × 100. Oblique lines are oriented ±30° from the manually identified AP axis. These photo-derived metrics are not equivalent to calibrated measurements.
